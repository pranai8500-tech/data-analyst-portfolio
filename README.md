## Analytical Workflow

1. **Understand the business problem** — Define objectives and business questions.
2. **Prepare the data** — Check data quality, missing values, duplicates, and data types.
3. **Analyze the data** — Apply SQL, Python, aggregations, and exploratory analysis.
4. **Measure performance** — Define KPIs and compare relevant segments and trends.
5. **Visualize insights** — Build dashboards and reports that communicate findings.
6. **Recommend actions** — Connect analytical results to practical business decisions.
